# Demo video

`tether-demo-draft-1.mp4`: 29.5 seconds, 1280 x 720, H.264, no sound. Made on Sunday 15 November 2026 at about 14:15.

## What it shows

The live public site (https://sam-tel.github.io/Not-so-lost-on-Mars/), following the shot list in [../PROJECT_PAGE.md](../PROJECT_PAGE.md):

| Seconds | On screen | Subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 4 | The globe of Mars with the site markers | Can I do this science and still get home? |
| 4 to 9 | Fly into Bright Angel | A real place in Jezero crater, at walking scale |
| 9 to 15 | The walk to Cheyava Falls; the tether line closing in as time passes | The tether: how far you can go and still walk home in time |
| 15 to 21 | Two stops; the verdict names the tightest point; the margin chart | Every point of the walk is checked against the clock |
| 21 to 25.5 | First person under the simulated visor | What it feels like on the ground (simulated) |
| 25.5 to 28 | The slope layer's information card | Every number has a source, a date and a range |
| 28 to 29.5 | Title card with the link | |

## How it was made

A script written by Claude Code drove the public site in a headless browser and captured its screen. A second script retimed each scene (1.2 to 4.9 times faster, because the test browser drew slowly), added the subtitles and the title card, and encoded the file. Every picture in it is the app itself showing real NASA, JPL, USGS, University of Arizona and ESA data; nothing in it is generated imagery. It is logged in [../AI_USE.md](../AI_USE.md).

Whether a recording made this way is acceptable under the rule "no AI-generated video" is for the team to decide. The safe alternative is to screen-record the same shot list by hand.
