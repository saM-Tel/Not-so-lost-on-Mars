## Fields (from the Project Submission Guide)

Every required field must be filled in or the form will not submit. All of it in English. No word limits are given.

- [ ] Project Name
- [ ] High-Level Project Summary: what we developed, how it addresses the challenge, why it matters
- [ ] Link to Project Demo: a video of 30 seconds or less, or at most 7 slides including title and intro. Public, no login. Over the limit means not eligible for Global Judging
- [ ] Link to Final Project: public, no password, permission or registration
- [ ] Detailed Project Description: what it does, how it works, benefits, aims, and the tools, languages and software used
- [ ] Use of Super Intelligence (the guide's name for AI): whether and how we used it. Not shown to judges
- [ ] NASA Data: exactly which NASA data and resources we used. At least one is needed for a Global Award
- [ ] Space Agency Partner Data and Other References: every dataset, resource and tool, including code, text and images, even open-source ones
- [ ] Box: agree to the terms and conditions
- [ ] Box: confirm all work is original and every external resource is listed

The page takes images (JPG, PNG, GIF up to 10 MB) but not videos or documents.

## AI rules to meet (Project Submission Guide)

- [ ] Our statement says truthfully where the idea came from; the guide says AI should not be used to come up with it

- [ ] No AI-generated image, video or audio anywhere. If one is ever used: a visible watermark on images and video
- [ ] No NASA logo, flag or mission identifier in anything generated
